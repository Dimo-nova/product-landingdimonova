# Case assets

Captures of Bálamo's own digital menu (https://carta.balamorestaurante.es), a Dimonova client
whose menu Dimonova built and runs. All taken with Playwright's Chromium.

| File | What it shows | How it was taken |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| `balamo-tablet-landscape.webp` | The wine list on a tablet in landscape | 1180×820 viewport at 2× |
| `balamo-phone-dish.webp` | The detail sheet for one dish ("Lomo de bellota"), photo first, on a phone. Used by the home showcase (`components/home/BalamoShowcase.tsx`) as the phone in front of the tablet | 390×844 viewport at 2×, captured 2026-09-10, resized to 560px wide |

Re-capture when the menu's design changes noticeably.
